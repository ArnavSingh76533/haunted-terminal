# Product steering
- Category: **Resurrection** — bring a dead technology (1980s dial-up BBS) back to life.
- Tone: eerie, a little funny, never gory. MORTIS is lonely, not evil.
- Every feature must feel authentic to 1987: uppercase system messages, 80-column feel, modem vocabulary (CONNECT, NO CARRIER, SYSOP, DOOR GAME).
- The demo must be finishable in under 3 minutes.
